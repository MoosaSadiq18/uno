How to play

Goal: be first to empty your hand. You score the points left in everyone else's hands (number = face value, action cards = 20, Wild = 50).

Your turn: play a card that matches the top card's color or number/symbol, or play a Wild. Can't (or won't) play? Tap the draw pile — you draw one card and your turn ends.

Action cards: Skip ⊘ skips the next player. Reverse ⇄ flips direction (acts like Skip with 2 players). +2 makes the next player draw 2 and lose their turn. Wild picks the color; Wild +4 also makes the next player draw 4.

Stacking mode: a +2 can be answered with another +2 (and +4 with +4); the penalty adds up and the first player who can't stack draws it all.

UNO! When you're down to one card press the red UNO! button. You can press it just before playing your second-to-last card too. If you forget and another player taps Catch before the next player plays or draws, you draw 2 penalty cards. Once the next player acts it's too late.

Voice & chat: use the microphone button to talk with the room, the speech bubble for text chat. Tap a player's mic icon to mute just them.
